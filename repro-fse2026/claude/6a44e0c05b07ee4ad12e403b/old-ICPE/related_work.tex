\section{Related Work} \label{sec:related_work}

\ju{Describe Kaltenecker et al.~\cite{kaltenecker2019} work.}
This paper is a replication of this study.
While this study analysis a wide variety of systems from different domains, we focus on the analysis of a single system.
%The previous work~\cite{kaltenecker2019} has considered different systems, however did not take into account the different factors related to each system that can change the results.
Compared to that paper, our study enables a deeper analysis of sampling strategies over the variation of inputs and non-functional properties. 
We analyze a set of seventeen input videos and two non-functional properties and as in~\cite{kaltenecker2019}, we collect results of 100 independent experiments to increase statistical confidence and external validity.
We aim at exploring if the results obtained in~\cite{kaltenecker2019} may be generalized over different variations of a single system
